## AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

---

## THEORY

Self-driving cars, also known as **autonomous vehicles**, are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence, sensors, cameras, and computer systems to perform driving tasks with reduced or limited human intervention.

AI enables a self-driving car to **sense its surroundings, understand the environment, make decisions, plan a route, and control the vehicle**.

### 1. Computer Vision

Computer Vision enables the vehicle to understand visual information obtained from cameras. AI algorithms analyze images and video to identify:

- Vehicles
- Pedestrians
- Traffic signals
- Road signs
- Lane markings
- Obstacles
- Road boundaries

For example, the vehicle can identify a red traffic signal and determine that it should stop.

### 2. Sensors and Perception

Self-driving cars use multiple sensors to collect information about their surroundings. Common sensors include:

- Cameras
- LiDAR
- Radar
- Ultrasonic sensors
- GPS

The AI system combines information from these sensors to create an understanding of the vehicle's surroundings. This process is known as **sensor fusion**.

### 3. Machine Learning

Machine Learning allows the vehicle's AI system to learn patterns from large amounts of driving data. It can be used for object detection, road recognition, prediction of other road users, and improving driving decisions.

### 4. Decision Making

After understanding the environment, AI determines what action the vehicle should take. For example, the system may decide to:

- Stop at a traffic signal
- Slow down near an obstacle
- Maintain a safe distance from another vehicle
- Change lanes when appropriate
- Follow the road path

### 5. Path Planning and Navigation

AI-based path planning determines a suitable route and driving path for the vehicle. GPS and digital maps can provide information about roads and destinations, while AI helps select an appropriate path based on the current environment.

### 6. Vehicle Control

Once a driving decision is made, the control system converts it into actions such as:

- Steering
- Acceleration
- Braking

The system continuously monitors the vehicle and surroundings and adjusts these actions as conditions change.

# RESULT

Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The role of Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Sensor Fusion, Decision Making, Path Planning, and Vehicle Control in autonomous driving was understood.
